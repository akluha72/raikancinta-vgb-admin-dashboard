Lead capture widget — demo

Click the button. The form saves the lead to the dashboard, then redirects to WhatsApp (if WHATSAPP_NUMBER is set).

💬 Chat on WhatsApp

Embed snippet for raikancinta.com

Add a trigger element and the script (adjust the domain to your dashboard):

<a href="#" class="js-raikan-lead">Chat on WhatsApp</a>

<script
  src="https://YOUR-DASHBOARD-DOMAIN/embed/lead-form.js"
  data-api="https://YOUR-DASHBOARD-DOMAIN/api/leads"
  data-trigger=".js-raikan-lead"
  defer></script>

Remember to add raikancinta.com to CORS_ALLOWED_ORIGINS in the dashboard's .env.
